About Bftero

Bftero is a gaming creator, streamer, Roblox developer, and digital creator focused on gaming content, live streaming, Roblox experiences, PS5 gaming, Minecraft, GTA, and Rockstar Games.

Based in Nepal. Streaming weekly. Always building the next experience.
